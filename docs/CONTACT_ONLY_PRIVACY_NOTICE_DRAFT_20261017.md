## Information we receive

The website currently allows visitors to reach the clinic by telephone,
WhatsApp, or email. It does not submit an online appointment request form.
If you choose to contact the clinic, you may provide your name, contact
details, preferred consultation type and other information in your own
message. Please avoid sending diagnoses, photographs, medical records,
test results or detailed symptoms in the first message. Ask for a secure
way to share any necessary clinical documents.

We use information you provide to respond to your enquiry, discuss
availability or fees, make appointment arrangements, and maintain necessary
administrative records. Contacting the clinic does not itself make an
appointment. We do not use appointment enquiries for marketing without
separate authorization. **[CLINIC MUST CONFIRM THE LAWFUL PROCESSING

WhatsApp messages are processed by the WhatsApp service under its
applicable terms; emails are routed through Cloudflare Email Routing to
